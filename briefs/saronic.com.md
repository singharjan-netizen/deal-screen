# Saronic — first-pass screen

**Source:** https://www.saronic.com (public site only, scraped 2 pages)

## What they build
Saronic builds autonomous surface vessels in three sizes: Corsair, Mirage, and the 180' Marauder. It also builds Echelon, a single command-and-control software layer used to plan, simulate, and run one vessel or a whole fleet. The primary customer is U.S. and allied naval forces. The job is to put uncrewed vessels into missions "where people shouldn't have to go." The site calls the fleet "dual use by design" but names no commercial customers or uses.

## Sector and category
Defense tech / maritime autonomy (uncrewed surface vessels plus C2 software plus in-house shipbuilding). It's a better version of an existing category, but the vertically integrated shipyard (Port Alpha) pushes it toward a new-entrant shipbuilder play.

## Stage signals
- **Operational use:** Corsair rescued downed pilots in the Strait of Hormuz. The site calls this the "first known at-sea rescue by an autonomous boat." The customer and operator are not stated.
- **Program pathway:** Marauder was "Selected for MUSV Marketplace" (Medium Unmanned Surface Vessel). Contract value and award type are not stated. Being selected for a marketplace is not the same as winning a production contract.
- **Field hours:** 100,000+ nautical miles in real-world conditions.
- **Manufacturing:** Port Alpha, a "next-generation shipyard," has been announced. Its build status, capacity, and location are not stated.
- **Team scale:** 17 leaders are listed, including a CFO, a General Counsel, a Chief People Officer (joined July 2025), a VP Corporate Development (M&A), and a VP Global Expansion (buying facilities "around the world"). This is a late-stage org chart, not a seed-stage one.
- **Hiring:** "Saronic is growing" and there are open roles. The number of roles is not stated.
- **Funding, revenue, named customers, certifications:** not stated.
- **Partnerships:** not stated.

- Built for 15g+ shock loads. **[unverified marketing]** No test standard or certifying body is cited.
- Proven across 100,000+ nm. **[unverified marketing]** A YouTube video is linked, but it doesn't prove the total mileage.
- First known at-sea rescue by an autonomous boat. **[unverified marketing]** It's a specific, checkable event, but the site gives no third-party source.
- Echelon runs everything Saronic builds from one interface. **[unverified marketing]**
- Team depth in autonomy and production. **[verifiable from site]** The site lists named people with bios: the CTO and VP Software are early/lead Anduril engineers, the VP Growth ran Anduril's maritime business development, the Head of Manufacturing led SpaceX's Raptor production line, and the COO led engineering at Liquid Robotics (Wave Glider) and Terradepth.
- Corsair and Mirage specs: not stated.

## Risks and open questions
- **Buyer concentration:** Almost all evidence points to one buyer, the U.S. Navy/DoD. "Dual use" has no commercial proof on the site.
- **Program-of-record risk:** MUSV marketplace selection is an entry point, not a funded production line. Budget timing and competing bidders (Anduril included, given the team overlap) could stall the move from prototype to program.
- **Capital intensity:** A company-owned shipyard, global facility purchases, and an M&A function mean heavy capex. The site gives no funding or unit economics, so we can't judge burn or fit with our check size.
- **Regulatory / export:** Selling abroad ("allied forces," "around the world") depends on ITAR/export licensing. Autonomous vessels also raise open questions on maritime law and rules of engagement.
- **Manufacturing execution:** It's unclear whether Port Alpha is operating or planned. Going from prototype to production is the hardest step, and the site gives no delivery numbers.

## Five questions for the founder
1. For the Strait of Hormuz rescue, who was operating Corsair, under what contract, and how much of the mission was autonomous versus teleoperated?
2. What does MUSV Marketplace selection actually give you (contract vehicle, ceiling, first order), and what's the timeline to a Program of Record for Marauder?
3. Is Port Alpha producing hulls today? What is annual vessel output now versus the plan, and what are unit cost and margin by platform?
4. What share of the 100,000+ nm was fully autonomous, and across which platforms and sea states? What is your disengagement or intervention rate?
5. Beyond demos, which commercial customer or revenue line proves "dual use," and does Echelon get licensed on non-Saronic hardware?

## Screen verdict
**PRIORITY:** the site shows operational use (the Hormuz rescue), an early Navy program foothold (MUSV), and a team with proven autonomy and production experience (ex-Anduril, ex-SpaceX). The main thing to confirm first is whether round size and valuation fit the fund, since the site doesn't state either.
